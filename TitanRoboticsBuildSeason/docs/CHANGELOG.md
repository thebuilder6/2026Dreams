2026-09-29 — nav/sim/stuck: **four robot-freeze mechanisms closed, all four test-first.** Every change is a regression test that failed on arrival; no fix was written before its test. **The finding that changed a belief:** the original assessment claimed two contested trenches sever the field into disconnected halves. The two-argument `isTrenchBlocked` keeps its robot-agnostic meaning for `planTunnelRoute` and existing tests. **(B) Contested corridors no longer prune the graph** — hard `if (blocked) continue;` replaced with a 20 m additive penalty (`OCCUPIED_TRENCH_PENALTY_M`, above the ~18.5 m field diagonal, so a contested trench is chosen only when it is the only way through). A* always has a route and merely *prefers* the clear corridor. **(C) Wall pockets behind the towers** — the roadmap stopped at x=1.80 / x=14.74 while the tower posts sit at x~1.06, so a robot or fuel piece in the driver-wall band could see no node and got a silent empty path. The cluster-weighted scent depends on instantaneous heading via its `alignBonus` term, so comparable pieces trade places as the robot turns.
