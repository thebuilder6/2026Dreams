Does the overlap cause problems?

No — and the overlap is structural, not a bug. The low-clearance band is anchored to the raw wall face (TOP_TRENCH_MIN_Y = NORTH_CENTER_Y + WALL_Y_LEN/2 = 6.7903), so the 0.45 m inflation necessarily intrudes 0.45 m into it. That is inherent to defining a clearance zone on an un-inflated face, and is harmless here: it shrinks the drivable band rather than making anything unreachable.
But the band is narrower than the controller's cross-track gate. Usable centre band is 0.3787 m wide; TrajectoryController advances waypoints at cross-track < 0.45 m. A robot shoved 0.18–0.45 m off the lane centreline therefore passes the gate while its centre is already inside the inflated footprint. The planner self-corrects (escape waypoint, path=3 in the probe), so the cost is detour / potential lane re-entry churn — not a stall.
